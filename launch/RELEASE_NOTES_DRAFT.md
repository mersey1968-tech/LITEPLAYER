# LitePlayer — Public Release (DRAFT, NOT YET PUBLISHED)

**Picture First. Sound Uncompromised.**

LitePlayer is a free Android TV player designed for home-theater enthusiasts who care about accurate frame-rate matching, high-quality video playback and preserving supported multichannel audio streams.

### Highlights
- 4K playback on compatible Android TV devices.
- Automatic frame-rate matching (AFR) on supported devices and displays.
- HDR and Dolby Vision format handling, dependent on hardware and profile compatibility.
- HDMI audio passthrough for supported Dolby Digital, DTS and other device-compatible formats.
- SMB network playback with improved buffering for high-bitrate 4K files.
- Audio/subtitle selection and remote-friendly controls.

### Validated so far
The project owner confirmed smooth 4K SMB playback on an NVIDIA Shield TV after buffering changes, with satisfactory audio and picture output. This is **not** a certification of universal Dolby Vision, HDR or all bitstream formats.

### Prerequisites before public download
- Finalize redistribution licensing and notices for bundled code/dependencies.
